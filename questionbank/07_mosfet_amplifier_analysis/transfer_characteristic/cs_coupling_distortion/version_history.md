# Common Source Amplifier with Coupling Capacitors

Concepts: common-source, small-signal-model, signal-swing, mosfet-dc-bias

## Version History

### v0 - Initial version
- Author: Jeff Stribling
- Date: Summer 2025 (first use)
- Content: CS amplifier with large coupling capacitors (V_DD = 10 V, V_DD/4 across R_D): gain as a function of V_OV, small-signal amplitude limit for acceptable distortion, change in drain current, and biasing for a 17 dB gain.

### v1 - Updated
- Author: Ammon Wolfert
- Date: Summer 2026 (first use)
